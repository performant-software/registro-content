    content: |
      Through public records preserved by the National Archives and Records Administration, fellows of Taller Entre Aguas (TEA), have spent two years transcribing and analyzing the 1872 Registro Central de Esclavos. We now have a dataset of more than 1,400 entries with information about enslaved people from the towns of San Juan, Trujillo Alto, Trujillo Bajo, Naranjito, and Dorado. Our DH project will create an accessible webpage where scholars, community organizations, and other folks interested in Black Puerto Rican history can search through names, regions, and other criteria, the people in these records. The webpage will be accompanied by historical background, charts, and images to provide visitors with the tools for an informed engagement.
