      Taller Entre Aguas (TEA) is Black Puerto Rican microlab in the Community Knowledge Lab, an archipelago of the Diaspora Solidarities Lab (dslprojects.org). TEA is comprised of three main projects: The Registro, the Criadas Project, and the Libertos Project. We are interested in expanding the ways Black Puerto Ricans gather and think together towards liberation, progress and action. These forms may include methodologies, curriculum, and other documentations for public use that offer best practices for decolonial work. We also aim to engage digital humanities through a diasporic, Black and ethnic studies approach.
